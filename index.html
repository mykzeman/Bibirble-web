help How to Play

Guess the verse by picking the book, then typing the chapter:verse digits on the keypad. Tap keyboard_return to submit.

  • Green: right book, or right digit in the right place.
  • Yellow: a book in the same area, or a digit in the wrong place.
  • Gray: wrong book area, or a digit not in the answer.

Daily challenges refresh at midnight your time and must be finished once started. Random games can be replayed by seed. Turn on "Useful verses only" to skip genealogies and lists.
